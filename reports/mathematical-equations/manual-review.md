# Mathematical Equations manual quality review

Reviewed artifacts: 15 Easy, 15 Medium, and 15 Hard deterministic samples generated by the 200-per-difficulty audit.

## Review method

Each contact sheet was inspected for equation legibility, clipping, response-box layout, arithmetic tractability, structural repetition, and the amount of dependency reasoning required. The review uses the generated family, dependency depth, and estimated meaningful-step metadata; it does not treat larger constants as evidence of difficulty.

## Easy: 15 reviewed

| Samples | Families represented | Estimated steps | Judgment |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1-15 | chain, direct, reverse chain | 2-3 | Pass |

- All 15 use exactly two variables and two equations.
- The set mixes direct values, short substitution chains, reversed relationships, multiplication, and division without awkward arithmetic.
- The questions remain quick, but several require interpreting the relationship before substituting; the tier does not depend only on direct assignments.
- Equation order and variable roles vary across the sheet. No two cards in either 10-question audit session share a canonical structural signature.
- Visual result: all equations and both response boxes are readable, aligned, and unclipped.

Conclusion: the samples resemble short, legitimate dMAT systems and preserve a fair entry tier.

## Medium: 15 reviewed

| Samples | Families represented | Dependency depth | Estimated steps | Judgment |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1-15 | merged, reverse chain, triangle, branch, chain | 1-2 | 3-5 | Pass |

- All 15 use exactly three variables and three equations.
- The sheet contains five distinct graph families, including branching, recombination/merged constraints, triangular coupling, and reverse chains.
- Multi-variable equations occur throughout the sample. The student must track relationships across the system instead of merely following three independent assignments.
- Samples 3, 5, 7, 12, and 13 require five estimated meaningful steps; the remaining cards provide calibrated 3-4-step variation.
- Arithmetic remains mentally manageable while solve order is often indirect.
- Visual result: all equations and all three response boxes are readable, aligned, and unclipped.

Conclusion: Medium is materially more interdependent than Easy and requires sustained substitution or relationship combination.

## Hard: 15 reviewed

| Samples | Families represented | Dependency depth | Estimated steps | Judgment |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1-15 | star, mixed, branch, merged, cascade | 1-3 | 4-7 | Pass |

- All 15 use exactly four variables and four equations.
- Every reviewed card contains at least one compound expression; the set repeatedly uses branching, recombination, indirect entry, and mixed dependencies.
- Ten of the 15 cards require seven estimated meaningful steps, four require five, and one requires four. The harder cards therefore require several dependent deductions rather than two or three isolated substitutions.
- The cascade sample reaches depth 3, while branch and mixed families provide breadth and recombination at depth 2. Star systems add whole-system constraints even when their recorded graph depth is 1.
- Constants and coefficients remain small enough for mental work; difficulty comes from connection structure and working-memory demand.
- Visual result: all equations and all four response boxes are readable, aligned, and unclipped.

Conclusion: Hard consistently requires whole-system reasoning and is visibly separated from Medium without relying on large numbers.
